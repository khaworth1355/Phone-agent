PRODUCT LINE OVERVIEW
---------------------
TEMCO manufactures industrial parts washer equipment, specifically spray wash cabinets designed for cleaning industrial parts and components.

PRODUCT 1: T-3 "LIGHTNING"
===========================

Product Category: Compact Spray Wash Cabinet
Design Philosophy: Offers the power and simplicity of the bigger T5 unit but in a more compact design

KEY FEATURES
------------
- Integrated Automatic Oil Skimmer
- Small Parts Basket
- Low-Water Shutoff (Protects Heaters)
- 6ft Power Cord with Plug & Matching Receptacle
- Powerful Horizontal Pump: 115 GPM / 65 PSI
- Heavy Duty Swivel Casters with Brakes
- Removable Tank Cover (Easy Access to Tank)
- Adjustable Thermostat
- Composite Turntable Drive System
- FREE Bucket of Detergent (TurboKlean brand)

TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS - T-3 "LIGHTNING"
-----------------------------------------
Turntable Diameter: 22"
Working Height: 28"
Tank Size: 32 Gallons
Turntable Capacity: 750 Lbs
Pump Performance: 115 GPM @ 65 PSI
Voltage: 240V 1PH
Dimensions: 42" x 30" x 54"
